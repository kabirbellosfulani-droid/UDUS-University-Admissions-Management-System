# University Admissions Management System
**Case Study: Usmanu Danfodiyo University, Sokoto (UDUS)**

The University Admissions Management System is a digital platform designed specifically to support and improve the undergraduate admission process at Usmanu Danfodiyo University, Sokoto (UDUS). The project addresses common challenges associated with handling large volumes of applications through manual and semi-manual procedures, including delays, repetitive data entry, misplaced records, calculation errors, and limited access to timely application information.

The system provides a central platform through which prospective students can create accounts, complete their admission screening applications, submit the required personal and academic information, and upload supporting documents such as UTME results and O’Level credentials. Applicants can also access their profiles to review submitted information, view their screening results where available, and check the progress and outcome of their applications without repeatedly visiting the Admissions Office.

For university administrators, the platform provides tools to manage applicant records, academic programmes, departments, and admission screening requirements. It supports the review of submitted credentials and the automated computation of applicants’ aggregate scores using the configured screening criteria, including relevant UTME and O’Level requirements. This helps reduce manual calculations and supports a more consistent approach to assessing applicants. The system also provides facilities for ranking eligible candidates and generating provisional admission lists in line with the configured programme requirements and available admission quotas.

The project is focused on the undergraduate admission process at UDUS, from post-UTME screening registration and document submission to screening, admission list preparation, and status notification. It is not intended to replace the university’s entire student information system or manage activities that take place after admission, such as course registration, hostel allocation, or academic records.

By bringing important admission activities together in one organised platform, the system aims to improve the efficiency, accuracy, transparency, and accessibility of the admission process. It also supports better record management and helps applicants obtain clearer information about their applications. Overall, the project demonstrates how a purpose-built digital solution can assist Usmanu Danfodiyo University in managing undergraduate admissions more effectively while providing a more organised experience for applicants and administrative staff throughout each stage of the process.
